# Glare - Discomfort Glare

> 分类：Solution home / Dictionary / G ｜ 更新：Fri, 24 Jun, 2016 at 11:56 AM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000077892-glare-discomfort-glare

Glare that is perceived solely as a subjective discomfort without causing any 
significant loss of visual performance. If it persists, however, discomfort 
glare leads to fatigue, and a reduced feeling of wellbeing and capacity for 
work.
