# How can I assign materials/textures to only one surface?

> 分类：Solution home / Construction / Materials ｜ 更新：Thu, 16 Feb, 2023 at 11:24 AM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000072832-how-can-i-assign-materials-textures-to-only-one-surface-

To change the texture or material of a single surface, you can simply use the "Apply material" function. It is also possible to assign the selected material to a single surface via drag & drop while holding down the "Shift" key.
