# Height above Working Plane

> 分类：Solution home / Dictionary / H ｜ 更新：Fri, 24 Jun, 2016 at  1:27 PM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000077905-height-above-working-plane

Height above the working plane is the distance between the Working Plane (usually 0.85 m from the floor) and the radiating surface of a luminaire. 

HLP = Height above the working plane

HWP = Working plane
