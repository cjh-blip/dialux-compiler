# Uniformity

> 分类：Solution home / Dictionary / U ｜ 更新：Wed, 29 Jun, 2016 at  9:12 AM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000078531-uniformity

In order to avoid problems of fatigue and to generate a feeling of wellbeing in an interior it is important to create a uniform distribution of luminance. This involves maintaining a certain uniformity of illuminance at the work plane, expressed as the ratio of minimum to mean illuminance. For the ambient lighting in office interiors, this ratio should be greater than 1:1.5 (EN 12464, Part 1).
