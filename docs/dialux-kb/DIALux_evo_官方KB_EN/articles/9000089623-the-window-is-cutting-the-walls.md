# The window is cutting the walls

> 分类：Solution home / Construction / Miscellaneous ｜ 更新：Tue, 30 Aug, 2016 at 11:46 AM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000089623-the-window-is-cutting-the-walls

When a window is placed in evo the wall is automatically cut to the same size the window has. Evo is cutting as long as no wall will be hit. If a windows is now for example placed ata a corner where two wall crosses it could be that the wall is cut on the complete length.

To avoid this behaviour it is possible to enter a fixed cutting depth and cut the wall manually.
