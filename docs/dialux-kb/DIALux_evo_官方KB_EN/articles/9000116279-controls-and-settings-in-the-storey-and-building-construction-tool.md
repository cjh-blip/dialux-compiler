# Controls and settings in storey and building construction tool

> 分类：Solution home / Construction / Storey and building construction ｜ 更新：Fri, 25 Mar, 2022 at 11:37 AM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000116279-controls-and-settings-in-the-storey-and-building-construction-tool

Active storey
Here you can give the "Active storey" a different name and add a description.

Properties
In the properties you can change the storey height and the storey thickness of the selected storey.

Storey overview
Here you can see all the storeys of the project with their height.
You also have the option of deleting the top storey by clicking on the small cross next to it. 
However, this is only possible with the top storey.

Clearance height
Here you can see the clear room height of the floor. If, for example, you have inserted a suspended ceiling in a room, the clear room height is displayed from "X" meters to "Y" meters.
