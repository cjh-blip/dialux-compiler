# How to construct reflective surfaces?

> 分类：Solution home / Construction / Materials ｜ 更新：Fri, 17 Feb, 2023 at  9:31 AM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000065907-how-to-construct-reflective-surfaces-

It is possible to design reflective surfaces, such as a wall mirror, in our evo software. The following instructions show you how to achieve this:

Create a colour material of your choice under "Construction / Materials" with the following advanced properties:

Material type: Metallic 
Reflection factor: 90%
Reflective coating: 100%

You then use the "Apply material" function to apply this texture to a surface of your choice.

The reflective effect cannot be seen directly in the project. But if you create a raytraced image of this scene, the reflective effect will be visible.
