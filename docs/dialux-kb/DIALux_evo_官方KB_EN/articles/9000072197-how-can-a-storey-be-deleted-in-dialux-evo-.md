# How to remove a storey?

> 分类：Solution home / Construction / Storey and building construction ｜ 更新：Mon, 28 Mar, 2022 at  1:50 PM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000072197-how-can-a-storey-be-deleted-in-dialux-evo-

A storey can be deleted under "Construction -> Storey and building construction" by marking the top storey and then pressing the "cross" symbol. Only the top storey can be completely deleted. Intermediate storeys cannot be removed.
