Additional information on the operation of our online services DIAL GmbH

This additional information is primarily intended for administrators who are entrusted with the security configuration of networks. The lighting design tool DIALux evo 11.0 and higher requires online access for proper operation. 

DIAL GmbH operates its online services on servers within the European Union. If data is exchanged via these services, it is also stored on servers within the European Union. These follow the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
