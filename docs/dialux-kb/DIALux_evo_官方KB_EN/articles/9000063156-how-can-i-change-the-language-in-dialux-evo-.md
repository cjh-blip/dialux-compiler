# How can I change the language in DIALux evo?

> 分类：Solution home / Settings / General settings ｜ 更新：Mon, 20 Feb, 2023 at 11:50 AM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000063156-how-can-i-change-the-language-in-dialux-evo-

You can change the language in our DIALux evo software under "File -> Settings -> General settings -> Common". Please note that a restart of evo is required afterwards.
