# Actions in Storey and building construction

> 分类：Solution home / Construction / Storey and building construction ｜ 更新：Tue, 31 Jan, 2023 at  3:21 PM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000116276-actions-in-storey-and-building-construction

Draw new room
With this function you have the possibility to draw a new room either inside a building or anywhere in the site. Here you can then select whether an outer contour should be generated automatically. If this option is activated, you can still choose the wall thickness yourself.

Please read also the article "How to construct a room in an already existing room?".

Draw new indoor contour
After you have finished constructing a building, the "Storey and building construction" tool opens automatically, where you can draw interior contours, among other things.
With the help of the scales displayed on the left and top edge of the drawing, you can determine an individual wall thickness.
The accuracy of the scale can be adjusted by zooming in and out.
You can create a new room contour by setting as many points as you like. You can close the contour by clicking the "Close" button or using the "Close polygon" context menu.

Draw new outer contour
With this function you have the possibility to draw a new outer contour.

New empty storey
With a new, empty storey you determine the building contour yourself. This means that the storey can also be drawn larger than the storey below it.

While you are defining the individual points of the contour, you can finish the contour by right-clicking and confirming the "Close polygon" option.

Duplicate storey
This function allows you to duplicate a completed storey by selecting it.
You can decide which objects will be copied to the duplicate.
By deselecting the checkbox, you remove the objects that you do not want to be automatically transferred.

After you have finished constructing a building, the "Floor and building construction" tool opens automatically, where you can draw interior contours, among other things.
With the help of the scales displayed on the left and top edge of the drawing, you can determine an individual wall thickness.
The accuracy of the scale can be adjusted by zooming in and out.
You can create a new room contour by setting as many points as you like. You can close the contour by clicking the "Close" button or using the "Close polygon" context menu.
