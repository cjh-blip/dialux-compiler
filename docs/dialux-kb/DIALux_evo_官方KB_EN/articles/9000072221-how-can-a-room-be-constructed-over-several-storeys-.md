# How to construct a room across several storeys?

> 分类：Solution home / Construction / Storey and building construction ｜ 更新：Mon, 28 Mar, 2022 at  1:35 PM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000072221-how-can-a-room-be-constructed-over-several-storeys-

It is possible to construct a room over several storeys by cutting out the storey between the rooms to be connected under "Construction -> Cutout" and connecting the two rooms with this cutout.
