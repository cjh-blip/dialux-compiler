# Why am I not getting calculation results for the daylight scene?

> 分类：Solution home / Calculation / Results ｜ 更新：Mon, 14 Jun, 2021 at  2:06 PM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000139073-why-are-results-missing-after-the-daylight-calculation-

If you do not receive any results after the daylight calculation, please check whether you have selected "Fast" in the calculation settings. Please note that no daylight is calculated in this setting.
