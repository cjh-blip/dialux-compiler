# Deactivate snatching of lines

> 分类：Solution home / Construction / Miscellaneous ｜ 更新：Tue, 30 Aug, 2016 at  4:44 PM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000089675-deactivate-snatching-of-lines

Lines of for example of DWG files are sntach automatically to assists the user. Sometimes it is required to deactivate this function. You can do this by pressing the SHIFT key.
