# Discharge Lamp

> 分类：Solution home / Dictionary / D ｜ 更新：Wed, 22 Jun, 2016 at  3:38 PM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000077462-discharge-lamp

In gas-discharge lamps light is produced through the ionisation of a gas in the 
discharge tube. In the process the gas becomes conductive. There are 
low-pressure, high-pressure and very high-pressure discharge lamps. Discharge 
lamps offer a much higher luminous output than incandescent lamps. They are 
operatedwith Ballasts (for current limitation) and usually with an Igniter.
