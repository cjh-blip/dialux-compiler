# Actions in the Ceiling Tool

> 分类：Solution home / Construction / Ceilings ｜ 更新：Wed, 27 Apr, 2022 at  1:07 PM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000214620-actions-in-the-ceilings-tool

In our ceiling tool you have the possibility to automatically insert a ceiling into the room, to draw a new ceiling or to adjust the ceiling to the room:

Insert ceiling into room
With this action, the false ceiling is automatically inserted to fill the room.

Draw new ceiling
With this action you can draw the false ceiling yourself and adjust its size and position as desired.

Adjust ceiling to room
With this function you can adapt a false ceiling to the room if, for example, the room dimensions have been changed subsequently.

If you have selected an active ceiling, you can change some values for this ceiling. Here you have the option of setting the distance of shell floor and the ceiling void as well as the ceiling thickness and the wall distance according to your wishes. You can also decide whether or not to close the sides of the false ceiling.

Finally, the clearance height of the room is also displayed in this tool.
