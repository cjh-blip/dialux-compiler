# Glare - Direct Glare

> 分类：Solution home / Dictionary / G ｜ 更新：Fri, 24 Jun, 2016 at 11:06 AM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000077888-glare-direct-glare

Glare originating directly at the light source. The degree of direct glare depends on the size and luminance of the visible luminous areas of all the luminaires in the field of vision and also on background luminance. Direct glare is considered adequately controlled where the mean luminance of the luminaires at the critical emission angle of 45° to 85° does not exceed the values of the luminance limiting curves.

BK = critical emission angle
