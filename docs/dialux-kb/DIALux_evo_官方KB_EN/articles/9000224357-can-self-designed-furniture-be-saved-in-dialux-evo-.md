# Can self-designed furniture be saved in DIALux evo?

> 分类：Solution home / Construction / Furniture and Objects ｜ 更新：Mon, 13 Feb, 2023 at  1:24 PM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000224357-can-self-designed-furniture-be-saved-in-dialux-evo-

With DIALux evo 11, you can save your self-constructed furniture or other objects in DIALux. The furniture files have their own file format and can also be exchanged with other DIALux users. This means they can only be imported and also saved in DIALux from version 11.

The user interface of the furniture catalogue has been adapted to that of the luminaire catalogue. There are now search and filter options. By right-clicking or using the actions in the footer, furniture can be loaded into the catalogue or saved from it.

In addition, furniture can be saved by right-clicking from the tools "Materials" and "Copy and Arrange". Before furniture can be saved in Copy and Arrange by right-clicking, the individual parts must be combined into a whole.
