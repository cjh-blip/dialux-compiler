# How can a shed roof/sawtooth roof be constructed?

> 分类：Solution home / Construction / Roofs ｜ 更新：Tue, 26 Apr, 2022 at  1:15 PM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000214570-how-can-a-shed-roof-sawtooth-roof-be-constructed-

To construct a shed roof/sawtooth roof, you simply have to create a new, empty floor. On this top floor, you must then insert further rooms in the size that the shed roofs should have. Each of these rooms must then be assigned a monopitch roof by drawing the monopitch roofs on the rooms based on the room size. Afterwards, the floor height of this floor should be adjusted.
