# Can DIALux evo and DIALux 4 be installed in parallel?

> 分类：Solution home / Installation evo / Miscellaneous ｜ 更新：Wed, 7 Apr, 2021 at 10:17 AM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000062773-can-dialux-evo-and-dialux-4-be-installed-in-parallel-

DIALux 4 and DIALux evo can be installed in parallel on your computer without any problems. 

The two programs do not influence each other. You can even work with both programs in parallel.

The electronic catalogs of the luminaire and lamp manufacturers are supported by both programs.

If both programs run at the same time, luminaires and lamps from the catalogs are transferred to both programs.
