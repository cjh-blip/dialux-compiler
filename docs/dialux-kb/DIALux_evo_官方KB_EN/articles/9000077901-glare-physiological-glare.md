# Glare - Physiological Glare

> 分类：Solution home / Dictionary / G ｜ 更新：Fri, 24 Jun, 2016 at  1:13 PM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000077901-glare-physiological-glare

Glare that causes a loss of visual performance (e.g. reduced perception of 
shapes and capacity for discrimination caused by glare from the headlights of an 
approaching car at night).
