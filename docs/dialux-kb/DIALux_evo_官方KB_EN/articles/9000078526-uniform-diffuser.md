# Uniform diffuser

> 分类：Solution home / Dictionary / U ｜ 更新：Tue, 28 Jun, 2016 at  2:24 PM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000078526-uniform-diffuser

A uniform diffuser is a completely matt surface with equal diffusion properties throughout. Its luminance is the same from all angles of vision. Luminous intensity decreases with the cosine of the emission angle with the normal to the surface.
