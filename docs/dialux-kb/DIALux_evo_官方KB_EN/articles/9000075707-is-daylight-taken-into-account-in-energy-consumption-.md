# Is the real daylight taken into account during the calculation of the energy consumption and the costs?

> 分类：Solution home / Light / Energy consumption ｜ 更新：Thu, 29 Apr, 2021 at 11:32 AM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000075707-is-daylight-taken-into-account-in-energy-consumption-

The energy estimation currently does not take into account the actual daylight. Depending on the room situation, the energy saving due to daylight can be more or less. 

This range is shown in evo's energy estimation: At least amount X of the artificial light energy consumption can be saved, but maximum amount Y.
