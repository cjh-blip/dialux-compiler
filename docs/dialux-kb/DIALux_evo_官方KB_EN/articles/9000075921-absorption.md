Light that falls on a surface can either be absorbed, transmitted or reflected, depending on the properties of 
the material involved. Reflection factors (r), absorption factors (a) and 
transmission factors (t) can be between 0 and 1 (0 -100%). The sum of the three 
factor is always 1. The transmitted or reflected radiation can be regular (1), 
scattered (2) or completely diffuse (3).
