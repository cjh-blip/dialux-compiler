# What means the option "Direct sunlight"?

> 分类：Solution home / Light / Daylight ｜ 更新：Tue, 4 May, 2021 at  3:23 PM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000089656-what-does-the-option-direct-sunlight-mean-

If this option is deactivated, direct sunlight is not calculated.

The option is related to the (only theoretical) CIE sky models, which describes a clear sky without direct sunlight. This option is therefore a possibility for theoretical comparisons.
