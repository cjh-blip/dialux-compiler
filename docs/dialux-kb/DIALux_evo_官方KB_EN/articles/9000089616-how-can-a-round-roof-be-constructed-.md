# How can a round roof be constructed?

> 分类：Solution home / Construction / Roofs ｜ 更新：Tue, 26 Apr, 2022 at  1:56 PM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000089616-how-can-a-round-roof-be-constructed-

There is currently no round roof in our DIALux evo software. However, there are two ways you can construct a round roof.

The first way is to cut out the standard flat roof and import and insert a round roof as a 3DS file instead.

The second possibility is to construct a round roof from the standard elements.
For example, a cube and a sphere can be used for this. With the function "Subtract" the lower half of the sphere can be cut away, as marked here in blue.
