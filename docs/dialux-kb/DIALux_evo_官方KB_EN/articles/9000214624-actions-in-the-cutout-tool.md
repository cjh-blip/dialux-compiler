# Actions in the cutout tool

> 分类：Solution home / Construction / Cutout ｜ 更新：Wed, 27 Apr, 2022 at  1:32 PM
> 来源：https://evo.support-en.dial.de/support/solutions/articles/9000214624-actions-in-the-cutout-tool

In this tool you have the possibility to draw a rectangular, circular or polygonal cutout. You can insert a cutout in the wall, the floor and in every wall, for example to connect rooms or storeys.

If you have marked a cutout, you can limit the cutting depth for this cutout to a fixed value.

In addition, you can of course manually change the position, rotation and size of a marked cutout.

Finally in this tool, all the points of a selected cutout are listed in a table, which you can edit in this table.
